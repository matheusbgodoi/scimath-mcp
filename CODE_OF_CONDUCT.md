# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces must remain respectful, technically constructive, and safe for participants of all
backgrounds. Welcome questions asked in good faith, critique ideas rather than people, document
assumptions, and treat numerical-safety concerns seriously.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized content, threats, deliberate
misinformation, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. The maintainer may edit
or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces where a
participant represents this project.
